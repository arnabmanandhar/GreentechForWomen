Greentech For Women

About Us

Greentech For Women (GFW) is a non-profit, non-political, and non-partisan organization based in Nepal.

"Climate justice is not possible without gender justice."

Founded

Established as a legally registered non-profit organization in Nepal

Mission

Advancing ecofeminist solutions at the intersection of gender and climate justice

Impact

Representing grassroots voices on global platforms and policy forums

Our Partners

Our Partners

UNESCO Paris
UNESCO is the United Nations organization that promotes cooperation in education, science, culture and communication to foster peace worldwide.
Thelma and Louise France
Thelma and Louise France works to benefit children and women around the world. It has participated in the development of the village of Baseri, Nepal. In Nepal, it finances children's education and helps women in difficulty.
Thelma and Louise Australia
Thelma & Louise Australia is a registered Australian charity founded in 2006 and based in Machans Beach, Queensland. It is committed to supporting vulnerable children, families, and communities—both locally and internationally—through a range of social and developmental initiatives.
Asian Development Bank
The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is a leading multilateral development bank focused on promoting social and economic development in Asia and the Pacific. It provides loans, technical assistance, grants, and equity investments to its developing member countries.
The Umbrella Foundation
The Umbrella Foundation is dedicated to supporting and empowering marginalized children and families in Nepal. Through education, healthcare, and community development initiatives, they strive to create a brighter future for those in need.
